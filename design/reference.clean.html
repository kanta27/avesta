The Avestagenome Project®

The world's largest systems-biology study of a single community — decoding longevity.

A landmark study of the Parsi-Zoroastrian population — among the longest-lived, most genetically distinct communities on earth — undertaken to understand the molecular basis of longevity and the rise of age-related disease.

  • 01
    A living biobankBlood samples and longitudinal patient data from a first cohort of 4,600, expanding toward 10,000.
  • 02
    Druggable targets & biomarkersA unique database of mitochondrial, nuclear and epigenomic variants linked to cancer, Parkinson's, Alzheimer's and rare disorders.
  • 03
    One of the world's most valuable datasetsMapped DNA combined with medical, nutritional and socio-economic data — fuelling drug development, diagnostics and global partnerships.
